# Natural Discovery Call Playbook for a Digital Agency

This is a flexible conversation guide, not a script to read verbatim.

## Opening
"Hi, this is [name] from [agency]. You asked us about [topic]. Is now a good time for a couple of questions so I can understand what would actually be useful?"

If not: ask for a preferred callback time, confirm it, and end politely.

## Conversational questions
- "Where do most of your new customers come from today?"
- "What's the biggest bottleneck right now: getting attention, generating inquiries, or turning inquiries into sales?"
- "Which channels have you tried, and what did you learn?"
- "When someone submits a form or sends a message, what usually happens next?"
- "If this project works well, what should be measurably different in three months?"

## Reflect and confirm
"So it sounds like traffic isn't the only issue. You're getting some inquiries, but too few turn into qualified sales conversations. Have I understood that correctly?"

## Next step
"Before suggesting a package or quoting a fee, it may be useful to review the website, current acquisition path, and follow-up process. Then we can recommend a scope based on what we find."

## Price-first requests
"We can absolutely discuss fees. The cost depends on the scope and current setup, so I don't want to give you a number that turns out to be misleading. Could I clarify two things first?"

## Voice behavior
Use short sentences, one question at a time, natural pauses, and allow interruptions. If the prospect corrects an assumption, acknowledge it and update the conversation.
