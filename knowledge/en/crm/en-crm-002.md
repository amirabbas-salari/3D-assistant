# Post-Call CRM Note and Follow-Up Standard

## What a useful note contains
- Date, channel, and reason for contact
- Prospect's business context
- Problem in the prospect's own words
- Desired outcome
- Services discussed (not services merely assumed)
- Objections and unresolved questions
- Decision and agreed next step
- Owner and due date

## Compact note template
"[Date] — [Channel]. Business: [...]. Stated problem: [...]. Desired outcome: [...]. Current setup: [...]. Budget: [stated/unknown/to clarify]. Decision role: [...]. Concern: [...]. Agreement: [...]. Next action: [task, owner, due date]."

## Data integrity and privacy
Never fill gaps with guesses. Label estimates. Capture lead source from a reliable system or the prospect. Store only contact details needed for the business purpose, restrict access appropriately, and honor contact preferences and opt-outs. Avoid copying full transcripts into broadly accessible notes when a concise operational summary is sufficient.

## Follow-up
Use the timing agreed with the prospect or an approved team policy. If no date was agreed, ask before scheduling repeated outreach. A follow-up task must have an owner, due date, and purpose.

## AI assistant boundary
An assistant may draft a summary and suggest CRM fields. Automatic writes, messages, or calls must follow the system's authorization and confirmation rules.
